SOKOBAN LEVELS

The goal of the game is to push all boxes onto the goal squares.

Only one box can be pushed at a time, and boxes cannot be pulled.

Character Legend:

'#' : Wall (Impassable barrier)

'@' : Player (Sokoban) on Floor

'+' : Player (Sokoban) on Goal Square

'$' : Box on Floor (Unplaced box)

'*' : Box on Goal Square (Placed box)

'.' : Goal Square (Empty target location)

' ': Floor (Empty navigable space)
